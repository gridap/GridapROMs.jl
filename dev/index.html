GridapROMs.jl

GridapROMs provides tools for the reduced order modelling (ROM) of parameterised partial differential equations (PDEs). It is built on top of Gridap: parameterised problems are written as in Gridap, with the parameter μ as an extra argument, and reduced with a few additional lines of code.

The library supports

  • steady and transient, linear and nonlinear, single- and multi-field problems
  • reduced bases built with POD or TT-SVD, in any norm, with supremizer enrichment
  • hyper-reduction with DEIM, S-OPT and radial basis function interpolation
  • global and local (clustered) reduced operators
  • distributed-memory parallelism of the whole offline and online phases

Installation

pkg> add GridapROMs

Reference

The library is organised in modules. The most common functions and types are exported by GridapROMs; the rest are accessible from the respective module.

ModuleContent
Utilsintegration domains, linear/nonlinear bundles, performance trackers, errors
DofMapsDOF orderings of FE spaces and sparse matrices
TProducttensor-product geometries and FE spaces
ParamDataStructuresparameter spaces, realisations, parameterised arrays and snapshots
ParamAlgebraparameterised algebraic operators and solvers
ParamGeometryparameterised geometries
ParamFESpacesparameterised FE spaces and functions
ParamSteadysteady parameterised FE operators
ParamODEstransient parameterised FE operators and time-marching schemes
Extensionsextension of solutions from active to background domains
RBSteadyreduction, hyper-reduction and reduced operators for steady problems
RBTransienttheir space-time extension for transient problems
Distributeddistributed-memory parallelism
